AI-Powered B2B Marketing Platform

Intelligent lead discovery and outreach automation

An AI-driven platform for discovering prospects, generating personalized outreach, and automating campaign delivery through email and contact forms.

Project overview

This project is an AI-driven marketing automation platform designed to help businesses perform targeted B2B outreach at scale by automatically discovering companies within a specific market segment and executing personalized communication campaigns through email and website contact forms.

The system combines lead discovery, AI content generation, workflow scheduling, and automated outreach execution to reduce manual marketing effort while maintaining delivery accuracy and personalization.

For a client, this means a single platform that can identify qualified prospects, understand the context of each business, generate relevant outreach, and manage campaign execution with clear visibility into what was sent, what failed, and what needs follow-up.

Business process chart

The platform follows a simple business flow: understand the market, choose the right companies, send the right message, and measure the response so the next campaign gets better.

1
Set the campaign goal

Choose the service, target industry, location, and the business result you want to achieve.

2
Find the right businesses

The system looks for companies that match the target profile and collects their websites and contact details.

3
Understand each prospect

The platform reviews the company website and available business signals to prepare a message that feels relevant.

4
Create the outreach message

It writes a personalized email or contact form message that matches the company’s needs and tone.

5
Send, monitor, improve

Messages go out on a planned schedule, then the team can see what worked, what failed, and what to follow up on.

Core capabilities
  • Lead discovery by industry, location, and keywords
  • Website and contact data extraction
  • AI-generated personalized email campaigns
  • Bulk outreach campaign management
  • Smart time scheduling and follow-ups
  • Contact form automation for selected sites
  • Delivery monitoring and failure diagnostics
  • Opt-out, throttling, and reputation-safe sending controls
Technical stack
Backend: Node.js / Golang microservices
Queue workers: Redis / RabbitMQ
AI layer: LLM content generation
RAG pipeline: Retrieval-augmented generation
Vector search: Embeddings + vector database
Orchestration: LangChain
Workflow graphs: LangGraph
Automation: Headless Chromium / Playwright
Database: PostgreSQL
Search: Elasticsearch
Lead indexing: Structured CRM-style records
Analytics: Campaign delivery tracking
Business value

Reduces manual marketing effort by automating lead discovery, research, and outreach execution.

Improves targeting precision through filtering, validation, RAG-based context, and AI-assisted personalization.

Provides campaign visibility with delivery tracking, failure diagnostics, engagement analytics, and follow-up control.

Cloud HR Track

Cloud-ready HR platform

Built to make department coordination clearer, simplify daily operations, and keep workflows transparent across the organization.

Project description

Cloud HR Track is a modern, cloud-based Human Resource Management platform developed from scratch using Node.js, Next.js, and PostgreSQL to deliver a simple, customizable, and scalable employee management solution.

The system was built to replace complex enterprise HR tools with a lightweight platform tailored specifically to the client’s internal workflow and operational needs. The client previously used Salesforce to manage costing and customization features, but migrated to Cloud HR Track to achieve a simpler interface, faster workflow, and full customization aligned with their business processes. The project successfully delivered that goal.

Project objective

To create an easy-to-use HR platform that removes unnecessary enterprise complexity while giving organizations complete control over their HR workflows and data structure.

Technology stack
Backend: Node.js
Frontend: Next.js
Database: PostgreSQL
Architecture: Cloud-ready & modular
Design goal: Simple, customizable, scalable
Core features
Employee data management

Centralized employee database for personal information, roles and departments, employment records, and performance history.

Recruitment and candidate tracking

Applicant tracking, interview stage management, resume storage, and automated recruitment workflow.

Employee performance management

KPI and goal tracking, performance reviews, feedback management, and progress analytics.

Employee communication and collaboration

Team communication, task collaboration, department coordination, and workflow transparency.

Key value
  • Simplified HR operations
  • Full customization control
  • Cloud accessibility
  • Lightweight performance
  • Business-specific workflow design

AI Autonomous E-Commerce Operations Platform

End-to-end intelligent commerce management

A self-operating e-commerce automation platform where AI handles search, orders, inventory decisions, and internal operations communication.

Project overview

This project is an AI-powered e-commerce automation platform designed to run the complete operational lifecycle of an online store, from product discovery and checkout to stock monitoring, team communication, and purchase planning.

Instead of manual coordination between teams, AI acts as a digital operations manager that improves speed, consistency, and decision quality across daily commerce workflows.

Core vision
  • Customers discover products through AI-powered intent search.
  • Orders are processed and routed automatically.
  • Managers receive real-time operational insights.
  • Inventory decisions are generated with AI predictions.
  • Daily store operations run via automated communication workflows.
System architecture
  • AI Product Discovery Engine
  • Smart Shopping Experience Layer
  • Order Processing Automation Engine
  • Operational Notification System
  • Inventory Intelligence Engine
  • AI Purchase Recommendation Module
  • Executive AI Summary Dashboard
Automation workflow highlights
1) AI product search

Natural-language and semantic search understands customer intent and returns highly relevant product suggestions.

2) Intelligent shopping flow

AI-enhanced cart, checkout, upsell, and cross-sell logic continuously optimizes conversion outcomes.

3) Automated order processing

Orders are auto-registered, prioritized, assigned to workflows, and routed to responsible team members.

4) Operational communication

Product, store, and customer managers receive automated summaries, trend alerts, and escalation notifications.

5) Inventory intelligence

AI monitors stock health, detects stock-out risk, predicts demand spikes, and flags emergency restock needs.

6) Purchase recommendation

When thresholds are reached, AI generates purchase summaries, quantity estimates, and supplier plan recommendations.

Technical stack
Frontend: React.js / Next.js
Backend: Node.js / Golang services
Architecture: Event-driven + REST + queue workers
AI models: Recommendation + NLP semantic search
AI engines: Demand prediction + automation agents
Data: PostgreSQL + Redis + Elasticsearch
Automation: Background workers + notification microservices
Infra: Docker + Kubernetes + cloud message queues
Business impact
  • Reduces manual operational workload across store teams.
  • Prevents stock shortages through predictive restock automation.
  • Improves order handling speed and process consistency.
  • Enables smarter inventory and purchasing decisions.
  • Automates internal communication and priority alerts.
  • Transforms e-commerce management into AI-driven operations.
Key innovation

Traditional commerce systems focus on transactions. This platform introduces AI as an autonomous business operator that coordinates customers, inventory, staff communication, and purchasing decisions as one connected intelligence layer.

Lienexpos

Complete CRM and business automation platform

Unified ecosystem with real-time synchronization across operations, finance, customer engagement, and decision intelligence.

Project overview

Lienexpos is a comprehensive enterprise-grade CRM and business automation ecosystem designed to centralize and automate multiple business operations within a single unified platform.

The system integrates POS, inventory, HRM, accounting, CRM, e-commerce, and analytics into one synchronized environment, enabling organizations to manage operations efficiently with real-time data visibility.

Project goals
  • Automate end-to-end business workflows
  • Centralize multi-branch management
  • Enable real-time data synchronization
  • Reduce manual operational errors
  • Provide actionable analytics and reporting
  • Support scalable business growth
Technology stack
Backend: Ruby on Rails (API architecture)
Frontend: Angular
Database: MySQL
Mobile apps: Ionic + Capacitor
Server: Nginx + Passenger
API: RESTful secure APIs
Core system modules
1. Point of Sale (POS)

Fast billing, barcode scanning, discount and VAT calculation, multi-payment support, and invoice generation.

2. Inventory management

Real-time stock monitoring, batch and lot tracking, low-stock alerts, multi-warehouse support, and automatic stock updates.

3. Multi-business and branch management

Centralized control of multiple businesses, branch performance tracking, and unified reporting dashboards.

4. Purchase management

Supplier management, purchase order tracking, purchase history, and auto inventory synchronization.

5. HRM (Human Resource Management)

Employee database, attendance tracking, payroll processing, leave management, and performance monitoring.

6. Accounting and financial management

Income and expense tracking, profit and loss reports, ledger management, and cash flow monitoring.

7. Reports and analytics

Sales analytics, inventory reports, financial insights, employee performance reports, and BI dashboards.

8. E-commerce integration

Online order synchronization, web and mobile platform integration, and real-time order tracking.

9. CRM (Customer Relationship Management)

Customer database, purchase history tracking, offers and discount management, and engagement tools.

10. Mobile and remote access

Remote system monitoring, mobile-friendly dashboards, and live business tracking.

11. Security and role management

Role-based access control, secure authentication, and data privacy protection.

Advanced integrated applications
Syncher app

Admin mobile app

Live sales analytics, profit and expense monitoring, and real-time decision support.

Order management app

Order lifecycle management and fulfillment tracking.

Membership app

Customer loyalty programs, OTP authentication, and promotional management.
